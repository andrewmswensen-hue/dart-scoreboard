# Dart Scoreboard

A phone-first darts scoreboard that runs in the browser and installs to your home screen.

**Play:** https://andrewmswensen-hue.github.io/dart-scoreboard/

- X01 (101 to 1001 or custom) with Straight, Double and Master in and out, sets and legs, first to or best of.
- Cricket: Standard, Cut-Throat and No Score, standard or random numbers.
- Dart-by-dart entry or turn totals, undo, and editing any past turn.
- Computer opponents at five skill levels (about 25 to 90 three-dart average).
- Optional house rule, **One Bust, Low Score**: your first bust is free, the next one locks you in, or tap Lock In any time on your turn. Everyone else gets one more turn and the lowest score wins the leg.
- Statistics with time filters, sector hit charts, and a full match history.

## Install on a phone

- **iPhone (Safari):** open the link, tap Share, then **Add to Home Screen**.
- **Android (Chrome):** open the link, tap the menu, then **Install app** or **Add to Home screen**.

## Your data

Players, matches and statistics are saved in the browser on that device after every dart. Nothing is sent anywhere. Use **Settings > Export all data** now and then to keep a backup file, and **Import** to move data to another phone.
